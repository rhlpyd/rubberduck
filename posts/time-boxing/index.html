Time Boxing

May 1, 2025 🕐 1 min

A lot of times I find it difficult just start. Getting to work would mean winning some battle to decide what to do and what not to do. That is not everything. It involves me to convince myself why do something and not some other thing. That is the real deal and that is where all the pain is. The mental pain involved in fighting our own motivations is huge and unimaginable. The only way to escape it seems is to just log off and come back to work some other time. Does this sound familiar to you? It happens all the time to me.

This is where time boxing comes into play and is such a handy tool. It seems if you commit your time up front you don’t have to face the painful battle. Time boxing does not one but two things to you. One, the obvious, it opens a specific path for you to do the planned task. Two, the less obvious and the most important, it shutsdown all other paths your mind can take you depending on your emotional state. Therefore, time boxing is important though it sounds like a simple solution to all your focus and productivity problems.
