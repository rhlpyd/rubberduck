On Writing

Apr 24, 2025 🕐 2 min

    • Prioritize
    • Follow a schedule
    • Stay organized

Writing a blog is a habit and like any habit it is going to take time to attain it. Honestly, I do not possess it. I would like to work on it going forward. Before anything else I want to first understand the following. What are the obstacles that I face to write regularly? How can I overcome those obstacles? What can be a practical plan to build the habit? I hope you as a reader will find it helpful as well.

Understanding what it takes

Prioritize

Writing takes time and effort to think, research and organize. I generally don’t commit that time and effort. It is really a mindset problem that I want to fix. Very often it feel writing is not as important and that is where the problem starts. The fact is writing has an great intrinsic value. If I don’t make a commitment to write then I am missing all the benefits of it. I am a knowledge worker and I just cannot afford to not write. Writing needs to be my priorty.

Follow a schedule

I don’t have a schedule and that is a problem. I don’t believe that writing can be adhoc. It has to be a deliberate effort. Writing is continous effort and never gets perfect. One should not wait for a perfect time and place to write. Just stick to a fixed schedule.The advice I give to myself is to just follow a schedule.

Stay organized

The very essence of writing is organization of thoughts and ideas. Keep your environment and life organized. With a mind that is mess it is hard to write. I need to keep myself very organized. It is easy said than done but the easy to way out to keep live a life that is minimal and simple. I do believe that minimalism is the only way to stay organized.

Be goal-oriented

Being goal-oriented is essential to form habits. It is what enables the above ideas and hence, it is very fundamental. Staying goal-oriented gives the much needed purpose, direction and clarity to write.

I will follow a daily schedule and follow all the above principles in doing so. I believe that I am going do very well indeed!
